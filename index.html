Definition 0.1

Lenin Angarita (n.) - a mathematician working on actions of the mapping class group and their restriction to the handlebody group, currently pursuing an MSc in Mathematical Sciences at Universidad Nacional de Colombia (advisor: Anderson Vera).

Also works in homotopy theory - including the CW approximation theorem and the invariance of homology and cohomology - and has studied the fundamental group of the Hawaiian earring, building on the work of Eda and of Cannon & Conner, toward a generalization of the group with a rigorous cancellation law.
