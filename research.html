Research

Research

My current work centers on actions of the mapping class group and its restriction to subgroups such as the handlebody group. I also have a solid grounding in homotopy theory, through the study of classical theorems of Whitehead such as the CW approximation theorem. Separately, I have studied the Hawaiian earring, working toward a precise description of its fundamental group - building on the work of Cannon & Conner - together with a rigorous cancellation law for this group.

Homotopy Theory: CW Approximation and Homological Invariance
Independent study

Study of foundational results in homotopy theory, including Whitehead's CW approximation theorem and the invariance of homology and cohomology under weak homotopy equivalence.

The Fundamental Group of the Hawaiian Earring
Ongoing study

Study of the fundamental group of the Hawaiian earring, following the work of Eda and of Cannon & Conner. Current work develops a generalization of this group, offering a precise description together with a rigorous cancellation law.
